Buoy's first release: a living pill in the middle of the GNOME top bar. It stays quiet while
nothing is going on, grows when something happens, and shrinks back when it's over. Hover it
to read more; click it for Home.

Needs GNOME Shell 50 (tested on Ubuntu 26.04).

## In the pill

- **Music**: the track and an equaliser from any MPRIS player; hover for the cover and
  controls; the Media tab for every player, seeking, shuffle and repeat.
- **Transfers**: file copies in Files and browser downloads (Chrome, Firefox) with their
  progress; finished ones listed in the Transfers tab.
- **Quick feedback**: volume, brightness, Caps Lock / Num Lock (and Fn Lock where the laptop
  reports it), input source switches, and a flash when you copy something.
- **Notifications**: shown in the pill instead of GNOME's banner; the Notifications tab lists
  them, and the ones you missed.
- **Claude Code**: each session's state in the pill; permission prompts and questions answered
  from the pill or the keyboard.

## When nothing is going on

- The pill shows the next event of the day, the weather or the time of day. Automatic picks
  whichever matters most and takes turns; scroll on the pill to see the others.

## Home

- One panel: what you missed, what is running, Now playing, the weather and what is left of
  today. The Calendar tab has the month and the week ahead.

## The top bar

- The app or workspace on the left, the tray and the system menu on the right, each on its own
  rounded background that follows the wallpaper's contrast; rounded corners on maximized
  windows.

## Bu

- Bu, the mascot, says hello the first time, shows the basics, and keeps you company in empty
  tabs.

Everything can be turned off or tuned in Buoy's settings (⚙ in Home). No daemon, nothing
sent anywhere: the weather comes from GNOME's own weather service.
