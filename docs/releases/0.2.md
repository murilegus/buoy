Buoy 0.2: a new look, and a pill that knows about much more of your day — meetings, timers,
your terminal, printers, networks, power, devices, your phone and your camera.

Needs GNOME Shell 50 (tested on Ubuntu 26.04).

## A new look

- **Sizes follow your text size.** The bar, the pill and every panel are measured from your
  font, so larger text makes all of Buoy larger, on every screen.
- **One shape for every hover**, the same rhythm in Home and the tabs, colours by role, new
  symbolic icons (Gravity UI), and motion in the Dynamic Island style.
- **Lights at the pill's ends**: the buoy light on the left for what you missed or what waits
  for you, the privacy dot on the right for the microphone, the camera or the screen.
- **Notifications as alerts**: a new one opens its card for a few seconds, then folds back.

## New in the pill

- **Meetings**: a meeting five minutes away opens its card with Join (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams…),
  then counts down in the pill.
- **Timers**: start one from the Timer tab; a ring counts down in the pill. Pomodoro too.
- **Terminal**: a command that finishes in a tab you are not looking at says so, with its exit
  code, its time and Go to Tab.
- **Print jobs**: the page being printed, the end, and the printer's problems (out of paper,
  stopped).
- **Networks**: Wi-Fi and cable connecting or dropping, a VPN coming on or off, and the sign-in
  page of a captive portal.
- **Power**: plugging in, unplugging, full and low battery, a slow charger, and the power mode
  with its three buttons.
- **Devices**: Bluetooth devices with their battery, headphones and displays plugged in, USB
  drives (safe to remove), phones and webcams.
- **Windows**: a window asking for attention on another workspace, with Go to Window.
- **Calls**: microphone and camera on together make "In a call", with Do Not
  Disturb until it ends (a setting).
- **Your phone** through GSConnect: calls (Mute), messages with a reply, one-time codes with Copy
  Code, rides.
- **Claude Code**: its questions — single or multiple choice, or your own answer — answered
  right in Buoy; Claude Code Desktop's plan.

## Home and tabs

- **The tab strip**: Home first, Calendar and Devices pinned, the rest in ⋯.
- **Device tab**: this computer, wireless and plugged-in devices, each with its page.
- **Calendar**: an event's page with its time, place, guests and Join.
- **Media tab**: one card as in Home, a chip per player.
- **Phone tab**: calls, messages and the rest from your phone.
- The battery tile shows the power mode, and a click lists the modes.

## When nothing is going on

- **Bu's eyes** stroll in the pill, sleep at night and look up at what you missed.
- **The battery**: its percentage and power mode, and for a minute after you plug in or unplug,
  the time to full or left.

## Bu

- **Bu says hello** when you log in: the time, the date and the weather, with a special day when
  there is one.
- New expressions for his eyes.

## Docs

- The docs and the design pages are now on the site in English and Vietnamese.

Two downloads, the same extension: `buoy@murilegus.shell-extension.zip` (what `install.sh`
uses) and `buoy@murilegus-v0.2.shell-extension.zip` (the version in the name).

Update with `curl -fsSL https://murilegus.dev/buoy/install.sh | bash -s -- --update`, then log
out and back in.
