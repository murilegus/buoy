# Code of conduct

Buoy is a small project made in the open. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives — is expected to be kind and
respectful to everyone else.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Assume good intent; ask before you conclude.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people. Say what would make it better.
- Accept that a decision may not go your way, and that "no" is a full answer.
- Respect people's time and privacy: don't share someone's personal information, and don't
  paste private material (notifications, file names, prompts) into public threads.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or deliberately making someone feel unwelcome.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination or slurs about anyone's identity, background or body.
- Trolling, repeated off-topic posting, or derailing a discussion on purpose.
- Encouraging or helping anyone else do any of the above.

## GNOME

Buoy is a GNOME Shell extension, and extensions.gnome.org applies the
[GNOME Code of Conduct](https://conduct.gnome.org/) to everything published there — Buoy's
name, description and screenshots included. Where GNOME's code goes further than this one,
it applies to Buoy too.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to **conduct@murilegus.dev**. Say what happened,
where, and when; links or screenshots help. Reports are read by the maintainer only and kept
private. You will get an answer, usually within a week.

## What happens next

The maintainer decides what to do, in proportion to what happened: a private word, a public
note, removing comments, closing a thread, or blocking someone from the project for a time or
for good. Whoever reported it is told the outcome.

This code applies to everyone, the maintainer included.
